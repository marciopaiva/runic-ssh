ADR-0066 · THE MAP IS A PREVIEW
A fresh install shows the classic navigation, four slots, and no map. The command reveals the shell selector in the toolbar, Classic raised; choosing Map swaps the whole shell, the rail becoming Home and Map. Turning the preview off hides the selector again, and classic takes over if the map was showing. Classic is the default because it is the finished half.
